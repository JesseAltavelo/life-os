Life OS: privacy policy and terms

Life OS is a personal app used only by its owner. It stores all its data on the owner's own phone.

If the owner turns on to-do sync, the app uses Google sign-in with the Google Calendar events scope to create, update and delete only calendar events that Life OS itself created for the owner's own to-dos. It does not read, share, sell or store any other calendar data.

The sign-in token is kept only in the owner's private Cloudflare account. No data is shared with third parties.
